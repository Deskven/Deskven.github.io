Font pairings

Pairing 1 · Playfair + Inter

Light, distilled

A serif display face sets an editorial, expensive tone; Inter keeps the body neutral and highly legible. The contrast in style does the work without shouting.

Pairing 2 · Space Grotesk + JetBrains Mono

Engineered clarity

A geometric grotesk pairs with a mono body for a precise, technical voice — well suited to specs, data and product UI.

Caption · build 2026.06

Heading hierarchy, done correctly

On restraint

Strong hierarchy comes from contrast in size, weight and rhythm — not from many type styles. One serif for headings, one sans for body, and disciplined spacing between them.

Spacing as signal

Generous space above a heading separates ideas; tighter space below ties the heading to its paragraph. The reader feels the structure before reading a word.

Contrast over count

Two well-chosen sizes with clear jumps beat five sizes that blur together. Limit the scale, increase the contrast.

C1 · MOTION

Scroll-triggered reveals

Elements fade/slide in as they enter the viewport, driven by GSAP ScrollTrigger.

gsapScrollTrigger

Engine: GSAP ScrollTrigger/elsewhere the lighter framer-motion Reveal is used

01

Enter on scroll

Each row registers its own ScrollTrigger and plays a fade + slide as it crosses the start line.

02

top 85%

Triggers fire when the row top reaches 85% of the viewport — just before it is fully in view.

03

Staggered

Rows are batched into a single tween with a stagger so the cascade reads as one motion.

04

Scoped

All selectors live inside gsap.context bound to a root ref — nothing leaks to global state.

05

Lenis-aware

ScrollTrigger.update is driven by the page Lenis instance, so triggers track inertial scroll.

06

Reverts cleanly

On unmount the context is reverted, killing every trigger and restoring inline styles.
